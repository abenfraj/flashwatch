The direction we are going with is this one:

DIRECTION D - AMBER TERMINAL

Monochrome. Amber on black. Everything monospaced. Rectangular frames with single
hairlines. No gradients at all. A barely perceptible scanline texture. States do not
change hue, they change INTENSITY: standby, normal, full-brightness alert.

Vocabulary to work from: amber phosphor, VT terminal, scanline, monospaced grid,
dim/bright intensity, box-drawing rules, character cell.

Why it fits: it is the cheapest of the five to paint, perfectly legible over any
background, and instantly recognisable. The risk is cliche - it needs real terminal
vocabulary (columns, table rules, intensity levels) and not just glowing green.

Commit to it fully. Do not hedge, do not blend it with anything else, and do not
soften it into a generic dark dashboard. I need two things, in this order.

FIRST, a token table I can paste straight into code. Give me exact hex values, one
per role, no ranges and no "or". These are the roles the app actually uses:

  field            the window's own ground
  field_2          a slightly different ground, for headers and inset areas
  panel            a card or panel surface
  panel_2          the second stop of a panel's vertical gradient
  line             a hairline separator
  line_strong      a hairline that needs to be seen
  ink              primary text
  ink_dim          secondary text
  ink_faint        labels, captions, disabled
  accent           the one colour the identity is built on
  accent_wash      the same accent at low alpha, as an rgba() string
  counting         a countdown that is neither soon nor ready
  soon             under 30 seconds
  ready            back up
  danger           destructive actions and errors
  overlay_panel    the overlay's backing, as rgba() - it sits over a live game
  overlay_rail     the chrono track's rail
  overlay_row      the tint behind one row of the compact list
  badge_backing    the opaque disc a spell icon sits on

Also give me, as numbers:
  - the overlay's backing opacity in play and at rest (two values, 0-1)
  - corner radii: overlay, card, row, pill, badge
  - stroke widths: hairline, panel border, progress ring, portrait ring
  - a spacing scale of five steps in pixels
  - the alpha of the painted shadow under the countdown text

TYPOGRAPHY: three typefaces - a display face, a body face, and a face for numerals
and small uppercase labels. Name real families that are free to embed (SIL OFL or
Apache-2.0 only; say which licence each one is under). For each, give the weights
used and the pixel sizes for: window title, card title, body text, small label,
overlay countdown, overlay role label. The numerals MUST be tabular (state whether
the family has tabular figures, because a countdown that changes width every second
is unusable).

TEXTURE AND EFFECTS: list what should be painted, and only things buildable with
flat fills, gradients, alpha, rounded rectangles, circles, arcs, clipping and
hand-painted shadows. For each effect, say in one line how it is built. If the
direction implies something impossible here (background blur, real box-shadow,
photographic texture), replace it with the closest thing that is paintable and say
what you replaced.

SECOND, and only after the table: generate one image. A 1:1 mood board for this
direction, divided into clear zones: the palette as labelled swatches, a numerals
specimen showing "4:23" "0:08" "READY" large, a few UI fragments (a pill, a card
corner, a progress ring, a hairline separator, a small uppercase label), and a
texture sample. Flat vector-style design presentation, no photography, no 3D, no
device frames, no logos, no watermark. Dark neutral background with generous
margins.
